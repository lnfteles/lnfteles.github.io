For the second semester of 2025 (July-December) I was the Teaching Assistant for professor [Daniel Levcovitz](https://www.ime.usp.br/daniel.levcovitz/) in the course ["Mathematics III" (CCM0213)](https://uspdigital.usp.br/jupiterweb/obterDisciplina?nomdis=&sgldis=CCM0213) for undergraduates of the Bachelor in Molecular Sciences.

Teaching Assistant responsabilities
------
In Brazil we use the term "monitor" for this position, and as the expectations for what a "teaching assistant" would do may vary from country to country I list bellow exactly what I did:
* I held weekly 1-hour sessions to help the students solve exercises and answer questions they had on the subject.
* I did not grade tests.
* I did not give any lectures.


Topics explored in the course
------
* Vector spaces, linear transformations and matrices.
* Determinants and systems of linear equations.
* Eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
* Elementary topology in R^n.
* Analytic geometry (coordinate systems, planes and distances).
* Differential calculus in scalar and vector fields.
