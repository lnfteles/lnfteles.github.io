1   Introduction

Welcome to my website! Many things here are clickable, so hover your mouse over things and if they get underlined, then you found a hyperlink. Hyperlinks that lead to other websites open new tabs and the ones that lead to another page of my website open on the same tab. If you wish to come back to the main page you can always click the title.

Here I host some papers, notes and blog posts, my presentations and academically relevant information (such as my teaching experience). I also share other projects of mine, math-related or not. Lastly, I have a section on interesting resources that I find might be useful (mostly done so I have a easy way to find them if I, or someone else, needs it).
